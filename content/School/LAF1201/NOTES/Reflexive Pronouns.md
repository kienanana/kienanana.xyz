Reflexive Pronouns are used when the subject and object are the same. They are formed by adding -self or -selves to personal pronouns.

| Pronoun     | Reflexive | eng             |
| ----------- | --------- | --------------- |
| je          | me (m’)   | myself          |
| tu          | te (t’)   | yourself        |
| il / elle   | se (s’)   | his / her -self |
| nous        | nous      | ourselves       |
| vous        | vous      | yourselves      |
| ils / elles | se (s’)   | themselves      |
