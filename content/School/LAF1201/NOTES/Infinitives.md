a verb infinitive is the basic form of a verb. for example, "to sing" is the infinitive form of a verb. you can recognise an infinitive in English as it begins with **to**.

in French, you can spot an infinitive by looking at the end of the verb. all French infinitives end in either **[[ER Verbs|-er]], [[IR Verbs|-ir]]** or **[[RE Verbs|-re]]** 

for example, the infinitive of the verb "to sing" is *chanter* in French. this is an example of [[ER Verbs]].
