# Les pronoms relatifs: qui et que

- **Link a noun to a clause that describes it**, avoiding repetition. The noun referred to is the **antécédent**.
- Both **qui** and **que** can refer to **people or things**. Choose by their role **inside the relative clause**.

## Quick guide

| Pronoun | Role in the relative clause | Usual pattern | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **qui** | Subject: does the action | qui + verb | la montre **qui coûte** cher |
| **que** | Direct object (COD): another subject acts on it | que + subject + verb | la montre **que j’achète** |

- **Qui ALWAYS stays qui**, even before a vowel: *la montre qui est chère*.
- **Que → qu’** before a vowel or silent h: *la montre qu’il achète*.
- These are quick patterns; the **subject / object role is the deciding rule**. Other words can intervene: *la montre qui ne fonctionne pas*.

## Check by rebuilding the sentence

- *La montre coûte cher.* → **La montre** is the subject → *la montre **qui** coûte cher*.
- *J’achète la montre.* → **Je** is the subject; **la montre** is the direct object → *la montre **que** j’achète*.

To join two sentences:

- *J’ai une montre. Cette montre coûte cher.* → *J’ai une montre **qui coûte cher**.*
- *Voici la montre. Tu portes cette montre.* → *Voici la montre **que tu portes**.*

## English equivalents

| French | English |
| --- | --- |
| la femme **qui** parle | the woman **who** speaks |
| la femme **que** je connais | the woman **whom / that** I know |
| la montre **qui** fonctionne | the watch **that / which** works |
| la montre **que** je porte | the watch **that / which** I wear |

- English uses **that / which for both roles**, so translating the word alone does not decide between **qui / que**.
- English can omit the object relative pronoun: *the watch I wear*. French still needs **que**: *la montre **que** je porte*.
