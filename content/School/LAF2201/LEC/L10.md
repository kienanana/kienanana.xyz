### Giving an opinion
- donner son opinion — to give one's opinion
- Je pense que… — I think that… (+ subject and conjugated verb)
- Je trouve que… — I think / find that… (an opinion; *trouver* also means to find)
- À mon avis,… — In my opinion,… (also *à votre avis* — in your opinion, already in the main bank)
- sembler + adjective — to seem: *Cet objet me semble pratique.* — This object seems handy to me.
- formidable — great / fantastic

## Les Pronoms Relatifs Qui et Que
> see: [[Les Pronoms Relatifs]]

![[Screenshot 2026-10-27 at 7.18.48 PM.png]]
- **qui** replaces the subject inside the relative clause; **que / qu'** replaces the direct object.
- Usual patterns: *qui + verb*, *que + subject + verb*. Other words can intervene: *un robot qui ne parle pas*. Both pronouns can refer to people or things.
- *Je voudrais une montre que je peux utiliser pour téléphoner.*
- *C'est un robot qui chante et qui danse.*
