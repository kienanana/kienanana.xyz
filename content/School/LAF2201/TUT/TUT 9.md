### La Durée
![[Screenshot 2026-10-30 at 12.50.23 PM.png]]

| Expression                   | Closest English equivalent | Usage                                                                                                                   | Example                                                                                        |
| ---------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **depuis + duration**        | **for**                    | Started in the past and still continues now. Use the **present tense** in French for this meaning.                      | J'étudie le français **depuis deux ans**. → I **have been studying** French **for two years**. |
| **depuis + starting point**  | **since**                  | Gives the starting point of a situation that still continues now. Use the **present tense** in French for this meaning. | Il habite en Italie **depuis 2016**. → He **has lived** in Italy **since 2016**.               |
| **pendant + duration**       | **for**                    | Gives a bounded length of time. Use the **passé composé** for a completed past action; other tenses are possible.       | J'ai habité au Canada **pendant six mois**. → I lived in Canada **for six months**.            |
| **pendant + event / period** | **during**                 | Says when something happens within an event or period.                                                                  | J'ai travaillé **pendant les vacances**. → I worked **during the holidays**.                   |

**Compare the same activity:**
- J'étudie le français **depuis deux ans**. → I've been studying French for two years; I still study it.
- J'ai étudié le français **pendant deux ans**. → I studied French for two years; that period is finished.

**Pendant does not itself require the passé composé.** It can also describe a future duration:

> Je vais étudier **pendant deux heures**. → I'm going to study **for two hours**.

**Memory aid:** *depuis* = “since / for, continuing up to now” in these examples; *pendant* = “for a bounded period / during.”

- **Adjective before noun** : make the liaison, e.g. vieux amis (z), petit ami (t), bon ami (n), bons artistes (z).
- **Adjective after noun** : generally no liaison between the noun and adjective in everyday speech, e.g. amis exceptionnels, artistes engagés.
- **Determiner before noun** : still make the liaison, e.g. des amis, des artistes (z).
