# Vidéo: Mes dernières vacances

## Consigne
Faites une vidéo d’une minute 30 secondes maximum (1’30’’). Parlez de vos dernières vacances avec des illustrations (photos, dessins, souvenirs...). Vous pouvez parler d’un ou plusieurs voyages.

## Grille de notation

| Critère | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Originalité et qualité de la vidéo | 4 |
| Pertinence, intérêt | 4 |
| Étendue et richesse du vocabulaire, correction lexicale | 4 |
| Morphosyntaxe et correction grammaticale | 4 |
| Maîtrise du système phonologique / aisance dans la prononciation | 4 |
| **Total** | **20** |

## English draft
on my last holiday, i went to japan with my dad and brother for 10 days. it was summer in japan, but the weather was really nice, not too hot. our first night, we went to a jazz bar, where we watched a live band perform. we also went to the aquarium, there were so many animals, like penguins and sharks - it was so interesting! in Osaka, we went to a baseball game because we love sports. there were so many people, and it was really noisy! we had beers and snacks, and i'll always remember this experience. in Kyoto, we visited a vinyl bar, owned by an old man named Momo. at this bar, you can give Momo a song request, and he will find the vinyl and play your song. i requested "Age of Consent" by New Order. in Tokyo, we visited a huge records store, where i saw and bought lots of cool CDs and vinyls. on our final day, we attended a live sumo show. i had never done this before, and it was really funny and exciting. the trip was lots of fun, and i didn't even mention all the good food. i will definitely be going to Japan again soon.
