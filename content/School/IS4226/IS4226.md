This module marks my first time trying a Finance programme elective, as well as my first experience in trading in general. 

> The module aims to provide students with the fundamentals of financial markets and best practices in developing systematic trading strategies and systems. Students will learn the differences between discretionary and non-discretionary trading systems, as well as the advantages of systematic trading. The course focuses on developing systematic trading models using financial data, technical analysis, and sound risk management principles. Students will also explore common trading biases and learn how to overcome them through data-driven decision-making and systematic approaches. Finally, they will learn how to design and develop algorithmic trading systems.

## Grading
1. Class attendance, participation, and activity submissions (15%)
2. Quizzes (30%)
3. Mid-Term Project (15%)
4. Individual Assignment (10%)
5. Final Project (30%)
