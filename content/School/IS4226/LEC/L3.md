### Quick Notes
- sharpe ratio = returns / risk 
	- take risk free returns = 0
- beta coefficient $\beta$ = cov(R, R_m) / Var(R_m)
	- volatility against a benchmark
	- percent allocation * beta -> volatility 
- $R_{i} = \beta * R_{m}$
	- LHS != RHS -> $\alpha$
- alpha coefficient $\alpha$
	- performance over the benchmark
	- $R - \beta * R_{m}$
- correlation 
	- eg. if 2 stocks moving together, why not invest all in the better one 
	- if correlated, minimising of sigma doesnt work
