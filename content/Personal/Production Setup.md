In case you are new to Music Production: 
- **DAW**: Digital Audio Workstation - a program you use for recording, editing and producing audio files (music-making)
	- eg. FL Studio, Ableton Live, Logic Pro, Pro Tools, Reaper, Cubase
- **Plugins**: separate pieces of software that live inside a DAW, usually falling under two main types: effects and instruments
	- effect plugins process audio, changing how the sound you run through them comes out sounding
	- instrument plugins create sounds (synthesisers) or manipulate recorded audio samples
